01 // THEORY CORE

Piezoelectricity is the ability of certain materials to produce an electrical voltage when they are mechanically stressed. In this project, sound waves create tiny pressure changes and vibrations. A piezoelectric transducer converts those mechanical changes into a small alternating electrical signal.

The signal can then pass through a bridge rectifier, which changes the alternating signal into a one-directional output. A capacitor stores charge, and an LED can indicate when enough electrical energy has accumulated.
