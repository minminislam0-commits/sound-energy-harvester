01 // THEORY COREPIEZOELECTRIC EFFECT

A piezoelectric transducer can create a small electrical voltage when its material is mechanically stressed. Sound is a moving pressure wave. In this project, stronger sound pressure causes the transducer to vibrate, producing a small changing electrical signal. A rectifier converts that alternating signal into one-way electrical pulses that can charge a capacitor.

SOUND PRESSUREAir pressure changes make the piezo element flex and vibrate.
MECHANICAL → ELECTRICALThe piezo material responds to stress by generating voltage.
RECTIFICATIONA bridge rectifier guides the changing signal into the same charging direction.
ENERGY STORAGEA capacitor temporarily stores the generated electrical energy.
